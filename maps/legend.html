GRID

Example: 15S YU 688 934 is a 100 m square. 15S is the zone and latitude band. YU is the 100 km square. 688 is easting to 100 m. 934 is northing to 100 m. Read right, then up.

COLLAR

Grid convergence is grid-to-true, east positive. Magnetic declination is the World Magnetic Model when the chart can reach NOAA. If that line says to use a current chart, do that. Do not invent a declination.
