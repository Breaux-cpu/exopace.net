Full-band survey & signal discovery

One rtl_power pass grids the whole 24 MHz – 1.7 GHz range and flags every signal it finds, strongest first. It fills in as the sweep advances (a full pass takes a few minutes) and holds the dongle until it's done or you stop it. Tap any discovery to listen.

Map — positions heard on the air

Aircraft over ADS-B, ships over AIS, APRS trackers and weather-balloon radiosondes all get plotted as they're demod'd on this board. Ships time out after 15 min, APRS after 30, planes after 2, sondes after 60.

Decoder

idle
Decoders pause the survival monitor (and each other) while they run. P25 follows the downtown Memphis simulcast control channel (Fire/EMS only); Meteor LRPT needs a satellite overhead (~15 min, 137.9 MHz); ISS SSTV needs a pass and takes a 130 s capture per picture; radiosondes launch ~11:00 & 23:00 UTC. WSPR decodes 10-minute transmissions.

Satellite passes — next 24 h

Predicted on the board with SGP4 from CelesTrak TLEs for the Millington station (elements refresh every 6 h). Tap load to set the decoder, then start it when the pass is up — or set SDR_AUTOSAT=1 (e.g. meteor,iss) to start a pass automatically.
